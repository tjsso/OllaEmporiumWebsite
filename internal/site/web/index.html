      A LITTLE BIT OF HANDMADE MAGIC

      THE HANDS BEHIND THE POTS

      Hello, I’m Jade.
      Lovely to meet you.

      I’m a Welsh potter living in the south of France, making uniquely colourful ceramics to brighten your day.

      I first discovered pottery at university, but it was through plenty of trial and error that I really learned the craft. Opening the kiln to find a piece that filled me with joy made me want to share that feeling with others.

      Today, I make beautiful, useful things to bring a little of that joy into your home.

      Jade
